Hair Treatment

Special, Premium and Prestige — three treatments matched to your hair.

Home/Hair Treatment

We check your hair first,
then care for it at the right level

Our treatments come in three levels: Special, Premium and Prestige. In a 1:1 consultation we look at your hair texture and service history and recommend the level that suits your hair now.

Combined with a perm or color, a treatment helps keep hair smooth and easy to style. If you are getting a perm too, see our perm menu.

Recommended for you if

  • Your hair feels rough after frequent perms, color or bleach
  • You want to care for your hair before and after a service
  • Your ends feel dry and hard to style
