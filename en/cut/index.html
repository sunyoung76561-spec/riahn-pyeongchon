Home/Cut

We look at your face, head shape and hair first,
then suggest an easy-to-manage cut.

Before every cut we hold a 1:1 consultation on your face shape, head shape, hair texture, skin tone and lifestyle. Head Director Yeondu focuses on layered, tassel, short and bob cuts and men's dandy cuts, while Team Leader Haru focuses on layered and hush cuts and men's hair consulting.

Want volume or waves too? See our perm menu.

Recommended for you if

  • You want a layered or hush cut that flatters your face shape
  • You want a change with a tassel, short or bob cut
  • You are looking for a neat men's dandy cut
  • You just need a quick junior cut or bang trim

FAQ

Cut FAQ

Which designer should I choose for a cut?
Head Director Yeondu specializes in layered, tassel, short and bob cuts and men's dandy cuts. Team Leader Haru specializes in layered and hush cuts and men's hair consulting. You can choose your designer when booking on Naver.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes. Junior cut ₩20,000, junior design cut ₩23,000. Prices may vary by designer level.
Can I get a cut or shampoo with hair extensions?
Yes. An extension (hairpiece) cut is ₩32,000, plus ₩10,000 for an extension shampoo. Shampoo only is ₩15,000, or ₩25,000 with extensions. Prices may vary by designer level.
