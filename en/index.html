About

A salon near Pyeongchon Station,
designed around you.

RIAHN Hair Pyeongchon Station is a hair salon at 2F, 43 Gwanpyeong-ro 170beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang, near Pyeongchon Station. Opened in 2020, our 15-chair salon has five designers offering design cuts (layered, hush, tassel and short cuts), women's perms (build, Elizabeth, layered, hippie and setting perms), men's hair (As, parted and down perms), volume straightening, repair perms, and personal-color-based coloring, bleach and balayage.

In a 1:1 consultation we look at your face shape, head shape, hair texture, skin tone and lifestyle, and suggest a style that stays easy to manage at home.

We are open all year except on the days of Seollal and Chuseok: weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00, with 3 hours of parking in the building garage (floors 3–6).

Why choose us

  • Five designers each focus on their own field: cuts, perms, personal and design color, and scalp and head-spa care.
  • 1:1 personal design that considers face shape, head shape, hair texture, skin tone and lifestyle.
  • Styles that are easy to manage day to day and keep their shape.
  • 3-hour parking support in the building garage.
