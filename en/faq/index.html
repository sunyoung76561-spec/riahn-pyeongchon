FAQ · Booking and Parking · Pyeongchon | RIAHN Hair

How do I book?
Book on Naver, by phone (031-387-2277) or by Instagram DM. On Naver you can choose your designer and time yourself.
Is there parking?
Yes. If you park on floors 3–6 of the building garage, we cover 3 hours of parking. Parking on the basement levels or the 7th floor is not covered.
What are your opening hours?
Weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. We are open all year and close only on the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
Do designers have different days off?
Yes. Eunchae: Mon; Taeeun: Thu and Sun; Yeondu: Sun; Dana: Thu; Haru: Mon and Fri. Please check exact schedules on Naver booking.
How are prices set?
Color, bleach, special perms, setting/digital perms, volume straightening, straightening + setting perms and repair perms follow the S, M and L prices by hair length in the price tables. Prices may vary by designer level and products used.
What happens on my first visit?
We start with a 1:1 consultation on your face shape, head shape, hair texture, skin tone and lifestyle, then suggest a style that stays easy to manage day to day.
Can I book a specific designer?
Yes — choose your designer on Naver booking. Cuts: Yeondu and Haru. Perms: Eunchae, Dana and Haru. Personal color and coloring: Taeeun.
Do you do men's hair?
Yes. Men's cut ₩22,000, men's design cut ₩25,000, men's cut + down perm ₩50,000, men's basic perm ₩100,000, men's color (cut included) ₩100,000. We also do As, parted and down perms. Prices may vary by designer level.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes. Junior cut ₩20,000, junior design cut ₩23,000. Prices may vary by designer level.
Where is the salon?
2F, 43 Gwanpyeong-ro 170beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang-si, Gyeonggi-do (경기도 안양시 동안구 관평로170번길 43, 2층), near Pyeongchon Station. The salon opened in 2020 and has 15 styling chairs.

Any other questions?
Call us or send an Instagram DM
