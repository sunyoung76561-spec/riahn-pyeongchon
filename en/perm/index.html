Designer Eunchae handles build, layered, Elizabeth, hippie and jelly perms and men's As perms; Director Dana handles build, Elizabeth, grace and vintage perms and men's parted and As perms; Team Leader Haru handles build and Elizabeth perms.

After a 1:1 consultation on face shape, head shape and hair texture, we aim for a style that is easy to manage and lasts. For hair care before or after a perm, see our treatment menu.

Recommended for you if

  • You want natural volume curls such as a build, Elizabeth or layered perm
  • You want to tidy men's hair with an As, parted or down perm
  • You are considering volume straightening for frizz or curls
  • You want a perm that is easy to style at home

FAQ

Perm FAQ

Does the price change for longer hair?
Special perms, setting/digital perms, volume straightening, straightening + setting perms and repair perms have S, M and L prices by length in the price table. For example, the repair perm is S ₩300,000 · M ₩400,000 · L ₩500,000. Prices may also vary by designer level and products used.
What men's perms do you offer?
We offer As, parted, leaf, shadow and down perms. Men's basic perm ₩100,000, down perm ₩30,000, men's cut + down perm ₩50,000.
Who should I book for a build or Elizabeth perm?
Designer Eunchae, Director Dana and Team Leader Haru all specialize in build and Elizabeth perms. Check each designer's days off on the Designers page or on Naver booking.
